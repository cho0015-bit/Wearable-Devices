The concept

Built for real life.

The Series 12 concept turns user feedback into practical design decisions.

01

More accessible

A more affordable approach could make useful wearable technology available to more people, especially students and families.

02

More useful

Improved health tracking could turn raw sensor data into simple, understandable information users can act on.

03

More comfortable

A lighter case and comfortable band could make all-day and overnight wear easier.

04

More dependable

Longer battery life means less charging and more continuous tracking throughout the day.
